# P10 Round011 — Ground-Arc Arrest kinetic chain

Round011 isolates one judged unit: whether Nyra Kestrel's Ground-Arc Arrest can read as a mechanically unified full-body deceleration rather than a static wide greatsword hold. Four identity-preserving 1536 × 1024 landscape edits were generated under a hard cap of four. Candidate 04 is promoted byte-identically as the strongest isolated pose proof for a fresh critic.

This is an original 2D concept keyframe only. It is not a benchmark pass, 3D authorization, Unity authorization, game-ready asset, or production-ready asset. Paid 3D and Unity remain locked pending fresh criticism and later authorization.

The accepted copy is byte-identical to `Iterations/P10_Round011_KineticChain_candidate04_reject.png`. The reject-labelled iteration filename is intentionally preserved as immutable bounded lineage even though the image was subsequently promoted as the strongest isolated pose proof.

Candidate 04 materially improves the event read over candidates 01–03:

- the huge right foot, deeply loaded right leg, and dropped hip form one dominant down-right arrest column;
- the rear left heel is fully airborne with a toe-only skid and small physical gouge;
- the complete hilt is lifted diagonally across the lower chest rather than held horizontally at the waist;
- the blade center of mass sits far outside the torso toward upper-right;
- pelvis/ribcage opposition and the whole-body diagonal make the blade mass visibly drive the pose; and
- cloth tails, straps, hair, and conductor lag coherently left, opposite the arrested blade direction.

Promotion is deliberately narrow. Native inspection also records these unresolved caveats:

- the hair reads as two opposed braided tails rather than the frozen single short asymmetric braid;
- scapular push-pull is improved but only moderate because both arms trend toward similar forward extension;
- the C-curve reads mainly through the full-body diagonal rather than a deeply compressed torso bend; and
- the broad blade and front grounding boot are exaggerated into giant block forms.

These prevent any claim that Round011 fully passes identity/design freeze or a hostile composite quality gate.
