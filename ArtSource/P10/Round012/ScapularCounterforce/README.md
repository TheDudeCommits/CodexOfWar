# P10 Round012 — Scapular Counterforce Lock

Round012 isolates one smallest judged unit: whether Nyra Kestrel's frozen Round011 Ground-Arc Arrest can gain an unmistakable upper-body scapular push-pull lock without changing the established lower-body load, rear toe skid, chest-height hilt, upper-right blade mass, identity, design, weapon, or studio lighting.

The builder target result is **FAIL** and the promoted image is **ineligible for the Scapular Counterforce Lock**. Four identity-preserving 1536 × 1024 edits exhausted the hard generation cap. Root approved candidate 04 only as the strongest bounded narrow shoulder-order/contact proof for a separate fresh critic. It is not a successful counterforce-lock pass.

This work is original 2D concept evidence only. It is not a benchmark pass, paid-3D authorization, engine authorization, game-ready asset, or production-ready asset.

Candidate 04 is the first output to create a clear pale gap between the jaw and viewer-left trailing shoulder and to place that shoulder beneath viewer-right. It also preserves complete widely separated hands, an exposed cobalt hilt span, upper-right blade center of mass, dominant planted right load, and airborne rear toe.

It still fails the judged target:

- both elbows are long and extended instead of one tightly folded and one long/open;
- the shoulder-height difference is modest rather than violently steep;
- no far down-left trailing-elbow return or triangular negative space exists;
- the torso C-curve and ribcage-versus-pelvis counter-rotation remain modest; and
- the action reads as paired hauling, not a push-pull arrested stop.

Native inspection also records design/contact caveats: the face and palette remain recognizable, but the chest harness/yoke, shoulder armor, hair response, and some hilt attachment details drift from the Round011 freeze. Both hand contacts are readable and complete, though their extended geometry reinforces the hauling read.
