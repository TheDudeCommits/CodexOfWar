# P10 Round007 — Nyra Kestrel original 2D concept

Round007 is frozen as a partial, fail-closed identity iteration. It addresses the prior round's largest gap by replacing the generic dark-armored swordswoman read with a visible storm-harvesting load path:

1. one split warm-ivory ceramic induction yoke around the left shoulder;
2. one exposed oxidized-copper conductor crossing diagonally into a right-hip capacitor;
3. one heavy right grounding greave with a broad flat sole and copper shunts, opposed by a lighter left leg; and
4. one blackened-steel storm-cage odachi with a small guarded mineral capacitor only at the blade base and a substantial insulated two-hand hilt.

## Required combat proof: not accepted

`P10_Round007_NyraKestrel_CombatGrip_v1.png` was intentionally not created. The initial edit and both allowed targeted revisions are preserved under `Iterations/`:

- The initial edit has a broadly readable two-hand grip but completely omits the defining induction yoke.
- Revision 01 restores the yoke, conductor, right-hip capacitor, and heavy right greave, but the upper hand is a hovering fist and does not enclose the hilt.
- Revision 02 preserves the identity anchors, but the trailing hand grips a separate copper element instead of the hilt, and the hilt crosses the leg silhouette.

Promoting any of those images would falsely claim a valid identity-preserving two-hand same-hilt proof. The set therefore remains incomplete and frozen for a separate fresh critic.

## Honest limitations

- The specific function-led thesis is strong in the accepted front/back turnaround silhouettes and in the weapon sheet, but it does **not** survive the complete required set because no combat-grip proof passes.
- The accepted turnaround's side profiles do not prove the heavy right greave as clearly as its front and back views, and minor braid, seam, and bracket details drift.
- The cobalt work-suit plus industrial hardware can read as a utilitarian science-fiction technician rather than a fully premium mythic-anime hero.
- The weapon's fasteners and copper route vary slightly between studies; its balance and stresses are visual proposals, not engineering validation.
- No benchmark victory, legal originality, exclusivity, or 3D readiness is claimed.
